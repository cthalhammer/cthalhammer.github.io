## Abstract

We establish upper and lower bounds for the off-diagonal entries of the capacitance matrix of infinite systems of spherical resonators in chain, screen and crystal geometries. Let *d* denote the distance between two resonator centres. For chains contained in a cylinder of bounded radius and with bounded axial gaps, the decay is of order *d*<sup>−1</sup> log<sup>−2</sup>(2+*d*). For screens contained in a slab and relatively dense in the tangential directions, it is of order *d*<sup>−3</sup>. For relatively dense three-dimensional configurations, we obtain exponential upper and lower bounds. No periodicity is assumed. Our proofs are based on the classical connection between the Dirichlet problem and Brownian motion, which enables us to relate the decay estimates to hitting probabilities of Brownian motion.
